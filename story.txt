So my sister kicked me off her family plan back in 2023. Fair enough, i was 26 and she was like "you have a job now, pay for your own shit." And i was annoyed at the time but whatever, i said i'd get my own.

Except i didn't. I tried the free trial for like three other services and they all sucked compared to Spotify's algorithm. Like, it actually KNOWS me, you know? I tried Apple Music and it kept suggesting country. I don't listen to country. I don't know why it thought i did??

Anyway, so here's where it gets bad. She has this family plan with her husband and their kid, and there's one open slot. I don't even know how but i still had the login saved on my laptop from before. One day i just... logged back in? And it worked?! I mean, wait, no — i knew it would work because i still had the password. I genuinely thought she'd notice though.

I told myself it was just for a week. Just until i found a better alternative. Actually, no — i never even looked. That was March 2023. It's June 2026 now.

The worst part — and i mean THE worst part — is she still doesn't know. She pays the bill every month and never checks who's on the plan. Her husband definitely doesn't care.

And every time we hang out she'll be like "oh i found this new artist, you should listen" and i'm just sitting there like... yeah i already have them in my playlist. Because of your account. That you pay for. That i'm stealing.

I keep meaning to just tell her. Or like, sneak off the plan gradually. But then she'll post something on her story about a song and i'll add it to my library and the guilt hits all over again. It's not even about the money at this point, it's just the lie. The lie has been going on so long that admitting it now feels worse than the actual stealing.

(and before anyone asks, yes i've tried to cancel myself. I can't figure out how without her getting an email notification. Which is a convenient excuse and i know it.)

I think the thing that actually bothers me most is that she was right to kick me off. I do have a job. I make decent money. I could absolutely afford $11 a month or whatever it is. I just... don't want to?! That sounds so shitty when i say it out loud. Or type it, i guess.
